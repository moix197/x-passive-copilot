# Spacehub features

Short list of what Spacehub (an unofficial, read-only community dashboard for LONG launchpad tokens) can
do. The digest uses it to tie post ideas to real features, so keep it accurate (one line per feature,
plain facts, no hype).

- Token list: lists every token launched through the LONG launchpad on Robinhood Chain, not only SPACEHOOD.
- Filters and sorting: filter and sort tokens by category, paired asset and market metrics, 15 rows per page.
- OG category: AI, SPACEHOOD, MOO and CLIPPY are flagged OG and grouped as their own category.
- Verified communities: a left-panel list and a /verified page of curated community tokens.
- Token page: price, market cap, holders, pools, a DexScreener chart embed and a section nav.
- Vault pages: shows what each token's vault holds (tokenized stocks), its value and burned amount.
- Vault LP module: LP position, projection and what-if cards for a vault.
- Milestones: verified tokens climb two ladders, Altitude (market cap) and Jettison (burns), with 25 goals.
- Token potential: a 0-100% score with a bar on verified token pages; "—" when not verified or not enough data (formula not shown).
- Home stat cards: four headline stats including launch counts and total market cap.
- Ecosystem sentiment: a 24h up-share dial with plain-language answers on buyers, activity and top 5.
- Events panel: Latest highlights (rare events: price steps, holder and vault levels, goals reached, newly verified) and Latest activity (burns, new pools, vault changes) on the Dashboard; replaces the old milestones panel.
- Platform page: ecosystem-wide metrics by section, launch log, fleet status and fuel mix.
- Share cards: Share button on tokens, milestones, vault LP cards and every Platform metric; each gets its own image card and the link opens on that metric.
- Watchlist: star up to 50 tokens with no wallet (saved in this browser); signed in, it follows your wallet across devices, and an offer adds this browser's stars. Left-panel tab, /watchlist page, phone bottom-bar button.
- Wallet sign-in: connect (Robinhood Wallet first) and approve one sign-in message; no email, password or fee; never moves funds.
- Privacy: one-time cookie consent banner, /privacy page, Cookie settings link in the footer; Google Analytics 4 usage stats, opt out anytime.
- Read-only and server-cached: the browser never calls third-party APIs; unofficial, not financial advice.
